Get to know us

About UniTrade

UniTrade is a student marketplace designed exclusively for Southern Luzon State University students.

Our goal is to provide a convenient platform where students can buy and sell school-related products and other useful items within the university community.

Our Mission

To make buying and selling easier, more accessible, and more convenient for SLSU students.

Our Vision

To build a trusted student marketplace that connects the SLSU community through convenient online transactions.

How to use UniTrade

User Guide

1. Create an Account

Register using your SLSU student details to access the UniTrade marketplace.

2. Explore Products

Browse categories on Home or open Explore Products to search, filter, and sort listings.

3. Add Products to Cart or Buy Now

Review product details, stock quantity, condition, and seller profile before ordering.

4. Checkout & Order Chat

Submit your order and coordinate your campus meet-up location directly with the seller.

5. Sell & Manage Listings

Use Sell Item to publish products with stock and condition, and manage them in My Listings.
